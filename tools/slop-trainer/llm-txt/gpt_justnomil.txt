My MIL has a habit of “redecorating” my house every time she visits.

At first it was small things. Rearranging kitchen cabinets because my system “made no sense.” Buying different throw pillows because mine were “too dark.” Reorganizing the pantry while I was at work.

Annoying, but manageable.

Last weekend my husband and I went away overnight for our anniversary and his mom offered to feed our cat. When we got home, our bedroom was completely changed. New curtains. Different bedding. She had apparently gone shopping and decided our room “needed warmth.”

I was furious. My husband agreed it was weird, but says she was “just trying to do something nice.”

The bigger issue is she also threw away several things she thought were “clutter,” including framed photos and a hoodie from my late father that I slept in sometimes.

When I called her crying asking where my stuff was, she sounded genuinely confused why I was upset and said “sweetie, you need to let go of junk eventually.”

My husband wants me to calm down because his mom feels terrible now and keeps crying about being “attacked for helping.”

But honestly I feel violated in my own house.

My MIL announced my pregnancy on Facebook before I even told my own family.

I’m 11 weeks pregnant after struggling with infertility for years. We specifically told immediate family early because we were excited but ALSO because we wanted support if something went wrong.

We explicitly asked everyone not to post anything yet.

Three days later I woke up to dozens of congratulations texts. Turns out my MIL posted a giant collage on Facebook saying she was “finally becoming a grandma” and tagged multiple relatives.

When my husband called her, she immediately started crying and saying she was “just excited.” Then she somehow made herself the victim because apparently we “yelled at her for loving her grandchild.”

I’m especially upset because I had a miscarriage last year and she KNOWS how anxious I’ve been this entire pregnancy.

Now extended family members are telling me not to “stress the baby” by holding onto anger. Meanwhile my MIL keeps sending nursery ideas in the family group chat like nothing happened.

I genuinely don’t even want to see her right now.

My husband’s mother still has a key to our apartment “for emergencies.”

Yesterday I came home from work early because I had a migraine and found her inside my apartment. She was in our bedroom folding laundry.

Not emergency laundry. Just normal laundry.

Apparently my husband mentioned we’d both been busy lately and she decided to “help out.”

I was already irritated, but then I realized she had also opened drawers while putting clothes away. Including my underwear drawer.

I asked her to leave immediately and she got offended, saying she was trying to help and that “family doesn’t stand on formality.”

When my husband got home he thought I overreacted because his mom “does this for everyone.” He says she folded his laundry all through college and it’s normal in their family.

I told him I don’t care if she used to wipe his ass too, she doesn’t get unrestricted access to our bedroom as married adults.

Now he says I insulted his family and embarrassed his mom unnecessarily.

My MIL wore an ivory floor-length gown to my wedding.

Not “kind of light colored.” Not beige. Literally ivory lace with beading.

Multiple guests quietly asked me if she was trying to compete with me. At one point during the reception someone genuinely mistook her for the bride’s mother making a speech and complimented “her dress for the occasion.”

The craziest part is she acted completely oblivious all day.

After the wedding my husband finally admitted he DID tell her beforehand the dress might be inappropriate, but she brushed him off saying “women my age can’t wear white-white anyway.”

I wanted to let it go because the wedding itself was beautiful overall, but yesterday she posted a Facebook album captioned “A mother’s special day ❤️” with mostly solo pictures of herself.

I showed my husband and even HE looked uncomfortable.

But now he’s asking me not to confront her because “that’s just how she is” and he doesn’t want family drama right after the wedding.

I’m starting to realize “that’s just how she is” actually means “everyone has spent decades enabling this woman.”

My MIL keeps referring to my baby as “her baby.”

I know some grandmothers say that casually, but with her it’s constant and honestly unsettling.

She says things like:
“How’s MY baby today?”
“Give grandma her baby.”
“You’re hogging my baby.”

Last week she got angry because my daughter cried when MIL tried holding her. She literally said “she spends too much time attached to mommy.”

Then yesterday she made a joke at dinner about how if my husband and I ever divorced, she’d “make sure her grandbaby stayed with the right side of the family.”

Everyone else laughed awkwardly but I honestly felt sick hearing it.

My husband says she’s joking and I’m reading too much into things because I’m a stressed new mom. But even my own mother privately admitted some of the comments are weird.

I don’t know if postpartum hormones are making me irrational, but I’ve started dreading visits with her because every interaction feels like subtle competition over my own child.

My husband and I bought our first house recently and my MIL has somehow decided this means she’s moving in eventually.

At first it was jokes like “save me a room when I’m old!” Then she started sending links to furniture saying it would look great in “her future space.”

Last weekend during a family dinner she casually told relatives she plans to live with us after retirement because “that’s what good sons do.”

I laughed because I genuinely thought she was kidding.

She was not kidding.

Apparently my husband and MIL had vague conversations YEARS ago where he said he’d “always take care of her someday.” Now she’s acting like living with us permanently was always the agreed plan.

The problem is my husband now feels guilty backing out because his dad passed away recently and his mom keeps talking about being “alone.”

I sympathize, but I absolutely do not want to spend the next 20 years living with someone who already criticizes how I cook, clean, dress, and decorate.

Now I feel like the evil daughter-in-law standing between a widow and her son, even though I genuinely never agreed to this future.
